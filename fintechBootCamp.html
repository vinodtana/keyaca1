Fintech Bootcamp by Pragyan

Welcome to the 30-Day Fintech Bootcamp presented by Pragyan, designed specifically for MBA students. This extensive program provides a deep dive into the world of financial technology, offering a blend of theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and real-world experience. The bootcamp is structured to cover the latest trends in fintech, including AI/ML, blockchain, digital payments, and more.

This 30-day bootcamp by Pragyan is designed to provide MBA students with an in-depth understanding of fintech, combined with practical experiences and industry exposure. Join us for this transformative journey into the world of financial technology!

Course Overview

Duration : 30 Days
